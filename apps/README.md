#### Penpot

Penpot is an open-source design and prototyping tool, similar to Figma, that runs entirely in your browser. It supports vector design, component libraries, interactive prototypes, and team collaboration. Unlike Figma it's self-hosted meaning your design files stay on the local server and there are no subscription costs or seat limits.
